WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Justrip JHS

Justrip JHS Tours And Travels is a professional travel agency located at Shawon Tower, Purana Paltan, Dhaka. We are committed to providing complete travel solutions to our valued clients.

From domestic tours across Bangladesh to international destinations like Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore, Dubai, India and more — we handle everything with care and professionalism.

Our experienced team ensures smooth air ticketing, visa processing, hotel booking and customized tour packages so that you can enjoy a worry-free journey.
